title: FAQ
description: Answers to what people actually ask before and during their first scan.
---

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Do I need to sign anything before Vulnix can test my domain?">
No separate legal authorization form — verifying that you actually control the domain (DNS TXT
record, an uploaded file, or a meta tag) is what gates whether a run against it can start. See
[Blackbox vs. Whitebox](/concepts/blackbox-vs-whitebox).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does my trial actually let me do?">
A one-time 25-credit grant, sized for exactly one quick Blackbox scan or PR review — no card
required. Whitebox pentesting requires a paid plan; PR review works on trial. See
[Billing & Credits](/billing-and-credits).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I downgrade or move sideways between plans?">
No — plan changes are upward-only today. Moving from Starter to Pro takes effect immediately, at
the new plan's full price for a fresh billing cycle (no proration).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my credentials during a run?">
Test-user credentials and custom headers are encrypted at rest and only decrypted inside that
run's own isolated sandbox — see [Security & Trust](/security-and-trust) for the full
picture.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Vulnix connect to GitLab, Bitbucket, or Slack?">
Not yet — they appear in Integrations as clearly-marked "coming soon" placeholders, not partially
working connections.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a public API?">
Not today. Report data is available as a structured JSON export per run, and SARIF for CI
pipelines — see [Findings & Reports](/findings-and-reports).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the difference between marking a finding 'fixed' and running Validate Fix?">
Marking a finding fixed is a status change you make yourself. Validate Fix is a real re-test — it
replays the original exploit against just that finding's asset to confirm the fix actually holds,
before you close it out.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Enterprise plans self-serve checkout?">
No — Enterprise is staff-provisioned only. Talk to us if you need custom pricing or a larger
credit grant.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

Question not answered here? See [Getting Help](/getting-help) for support contact and
