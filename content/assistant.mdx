**Ask Vulnix** is the button in the header of the portal. It opens an assistant beside the page
you are on. It is not the pentest agent: that one works on your targets in [Chat](/chat). The
assistant helps you use Vulnix itself.

## What it can do

- **Answer how-to questions** from this documentation. Each answer lists the pages it used, so you
  can read further.
- **Read your workspace**: your domains, repositories, pentests, findings, reports, plan and usage.
  It sees only what your role can see, and nothing outside your workspace.
- **Make changes**, such as adding a domain, starting a pentest, changing a finding's status,
  checking a fix or inviting a teammate. It shows you what it is about to do first (see below).

It answers only questions about Vulnix and your workspace, and it replies in the language you
write in.

## Nothing changes without your approval

Before any change, the assistant shows a card that says what it will do and with which values.
Nothing happens until you press **Approve**. **Cancel** leaves everything as it was. If the change
uses your plan (a pentest, a fix check, a fix pull request) or removes something, the card says so.

Every approved change runs as you, with your role's permissions, and is recorded in your
workspace's audit log, so an admin can see what was done and by whom.

## Your conversation

The conversation stays in your browser, per workspace. **New conversation** clears it. Each
workspace can send up to 60 messages an hour to the assistant.
