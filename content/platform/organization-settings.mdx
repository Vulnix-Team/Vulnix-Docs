## General

Your organization's name and profile live here — visible to every member, and on any report you
export.

### Deleting an organization

<Callout type="error" title="Irreversible">
  Deleting an organization cancels any active subscription immediately and permanently deletes
  every scope, run, finding, member, connected repository, and domain that belongs to it. There is
  no undo, and no recovery window.
</Callout>

To make an accidental click impossible, deletion requires typing the organization's exact name to
confirm before it proceeds — an `owner`-only action.
