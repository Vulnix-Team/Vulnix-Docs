Vulnix tells you about three kinds of things: what your pentests found, what needs your attention
(an assigned finding, a failed payment, a schedule about to run), and a few short guides while you
get started. Everything shows in the bell inside Vulnix. Some of it also reaches you by email, and
a few alerts can reach you as a browser notification. You choose which, per person.

## The bell

Every user has a bell icon with an unread count. From the feed you can mark individual
notifications read, dismiss them, or mark everything read at once. Older notifications load on
demand rather than all at once. The bell is always on.

## Choose what reaches you

Open **Settings > Notifications**. Each row is a kind of message, with a switch for email and, where
it applies, one for browser notifications. Your choices are yours alone and never change anyone
else's.

### What an email contains

Emails tell you that something happened and how much (for example, a pentest finished with
2 critical and 5 medium findings), then link you into Vulnix. They never contain the findings
themselves, evidence, or credentials. Open the link to see the details.

### Quiet hours and limits

Getting-started tips, catch-up emails and plan-usage notices are held between 9 pm and 8 am
in your own time zone (Vulnix picks it up from your browser), and you receive at most one of them a
day and three a week. Pentest results, assignments, schedule reminders, security and billing emails
are not held.

## Browser notifications

Turn on **Browser notifications** in Settings > Notifications to hear the moment a pentest
finishes, or proves a critical finding while it is still running, even when Vulnix is not open.
It is a per-device switch: turn it on in each browser you want. Your browser asks for permission
the first time. Browser notifications carry a short line of counts and a link, nothing else.

## Pentest-finished emails

The email when a pentest finishes goes to whoever started it, and is on by default for every scope.
You can switch it off for a scope, or add extra addresses to copy, when you set up the pentest.

## Unsubscribing

Every optional email has an **Unsubscribe** link, and mail apps that show their own unsubscribe
button work too. One click turns off that kind of email. You can switch it back on any time in
Settings > Notifications. Security and billing emails have no unsubscribe link because they
cannot be turned off.

If an address bounces or is reported as spam, Vulnix stops emailing it. If that happened by
mistake, contact support from [Getting help](/getting-help).

## Replies

Replies to any Vulnix email reach support at support@vulnix.dev.
