Every plan is two allowances: **pentests** and **PR reviews**. You always know what a piece of
work uses before you start it. A pentest uses one pentest and a PR review uses one PR review. Nothing
is metered by run time or resource use.

## What counts as one pentest

One full-depth pentest of a verified live target ([Blackbox](/concepts/blackbox-vs-whitebox)) or a
connected repository ([Whitebox](/concepts/blackbox-vs-whitebox#whitebox)), with or without a
verified domain also attached. They all cost the same: one pentest.

- **Always full depth.** There's no quick mode. Every pentest on every plan, Free included, runs at
  full depth.
- **Used at launch.** The pentest is taken the moment the run starts. A run refused because a
  precondition failed doesn't use one.
- **Given back if it fails.** A pentest that can't complete is returned to your allowance. One you
  stop yourself is not.

## PR reviews

PR reviews have their own monthly allowance and never use a pentest. Each pull request reviewed
uses one. A re-review after new commits counts as another review.

The month is your billing period on a paid plan, and the calendar month on Free. Once the month's
reviews are used, new pull requests are skipped (not queued) and you get a **PR review skipped**
notification. Reviews resume when the month resets, or right away if you upgrade.

## The Free plan

Free gives you one full-depth pentest, Blackbox or Whitebox, and 5 PR reviews a month. It doesn't
expire.

Free comes with the workspace you create when you sign up. Each workspace has its own plan, so a
workspace you create later starts with no pentests and no PR reviews until you choose a plan for
it.

<Callout type="warn" title="What Free holds back">
  Low and medium findings show in full. Critical and high findings show their severity and count,
  but their details, the agent trace, and a fix pull request need a paid plan. See
  [withheld detail](/api/conventions#withheld-detail-on-the-free-trial).
  [Chat](/chat) also needs a paid plan.
</Callout>

created before a plan change keep working.

## Monthly allowances and upgrades

Your pentests and PR reviews renew at the start of each billing month. Unused pentests don't carry
over to the next month.

You can upgrade at any time from `/settings/billing`. Plan changes are upward-only, with no in-app
downgrade or lateral move. An upgrade takes effect immediately:

- you're charged the new plan's full price, and a new billing month starts (no proration);
- you get the new plan's full allowance right away;
- any pentests you hadn't used yet come with you.

## Where to see your usage

`/settings/billing` shows your plan, the pentests you have left, the PR reviews used this month
and when they reset. Over the API, the same numbers are in
[`GET /billing/status`](/api/billing#current-status).

## Starter and the earlier Pro plan

Organizations already on **Starter** ($99/mo, 500 credits) or the **earlier Pro** plan ($299/mo,
2,000 credits) keep their plan, price and monthly credits exactly as they were. These plans are no
longer sold to new customers. Enterprise contracts are also billed in credits unless agreed
otherwise.

On these plans, each action has a fixed credit price, taken when you launch it:

can buy, are spent after them and don't expire. Chat on these plans is charged in credits for

write to support@vulnix.dev.
