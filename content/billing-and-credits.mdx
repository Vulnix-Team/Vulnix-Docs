title: Billing & Credits
description: Prepaid credits, flat per-action pricing, and how the trial works.
---

Vulnix bills on a credit system, not open-ended usage metering. Every billable action has a
fixed, upfront price, charged the moment you launch it — never metered by run duration or
resource consumption, so you always know the cost before you commit to it.

Credits are Vulnix's own unit, not dollars. You buy them with a plan or a top-up, and every
price below is quoted in credits.

<Callout title="Trial">
  Every new organization gets a one-time 25-credit trial grant — exactly enough for one quick
  Blackbox scan or PR review, no card required. Whitebox pentesting requires upgrading to a paid
  plan; PR review works on the trial plan too.
</Callout>

## Upgrading plans

Plan changes are upward-only — you can move from Starter to Pro, but there's no in-app downgrade
or lateral move. An upgrade takes effect immediately: you're charged for a full new billing
cycle at the new plan's price (no proration), and your credit grant becomes the new plan's full
monthly amount right away.

## Where to see your usage

`/settings/billing` shows your current plan, credit balance, and a full ledger of every
charge and grant — the same audit trail that backs support's ability to explain exactly what a
credit balance reflects.
