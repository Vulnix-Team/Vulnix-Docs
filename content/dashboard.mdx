just a single run.

## Time range

Every chart responds to the same range control, so you're always comparing the same window across
widgets rather than reconciling mismatched date ranges by hand.

## Trial organizations

<Callout type="warn" title="Full detail on critical/high findings requires a paid plan">
  On a trial organization, a critical or high severity entry in **Top Issues** or the
  exploitability list shows its severity and status, but the title and CVE are shown as a
  redacted placeholder rather than the real value — this is intentional gating, not a rendering
  issue. Every other widget, and every medium/low/info finding, is shown in full regardless of
  plan.
</Callout>
