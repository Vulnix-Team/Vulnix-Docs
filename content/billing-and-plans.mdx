Vulnix is sold as two things: **pentests** and **PR reviews**. Every plan includes a number of each
every month, and there is nothing else to buy on top. You always know what a pentest costs before
you start it, because it costs one pentest from your plan.

## Upgrading plans

Plan changes are upward-only: you can move from Builder to Startup or Pro, but there's no in-app
downgrade or lateral move. An upgrade takes effect immediately. You are charged a full new billing
cycle at the new plan's price (no proration), and the new plan's pentests are added to whatever you
have left.

To go lower, turn auto renewal off in Billing, keep the plan until the period ends, then choose
the lower plan.

## Startup program codes

Early-stage companies can apply for a code that takes 50% off a plan for 12 months. It works from
the account that applied, for one workspace. Enter it in Billing before you pick a plan.

## Where to see your usage

`/settings/billing` shows your current plan, the pentests you have left, the PR reviews used this
month and when they renew. Owners can change the plan, turn auto renewal on or off and open the
Stripe billing portal; admins can see the same numbers.
