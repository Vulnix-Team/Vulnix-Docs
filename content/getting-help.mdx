## Before you reach out

A quick pass through these usually answers the question faster than waiting on a reply:

- [FAQ](/faq) — the most common questions, answered directly.
- [Glossary](/glossary) — if a term in the product isn't landing.
- [Security & Trust](/security-and-trust) — if the question is about how your data or
  credentials are handled.

If you're reporting something that looks broken rather than asking how something works, include
the organization name, roughly when it happened, and — if it's about a specific run or PR
review — its ID from the URL. It's the difference between a same-day fix and a lot of back and
forth.
